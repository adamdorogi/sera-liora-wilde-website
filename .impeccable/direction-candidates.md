# Grounded visual directions

Mechanism: independent practitioners with distinct modalities, connected through a caring local collective.
Scene: a local visitor on a phone at home, looking for a grounded person and the next step.
Cultural home: Queensland gardens, community spaces, personal treatment rooms and bayside life.
Avoid the predictable beige wellness brochure and its neon-tech opposite.

1. Coastal companion: deep blue, open daylight, portrait-led service navigation; clearest continuity with the incumbent.
2. Garden fieldbook: leafy green, specimen labels and useful indexes; practitioner discovery as a growing local collection.
3. Portrait studio: bold, close human photography and calm captions; people lead every decision.
4. Tidal pathways: bayside blue, continuous horizontal bands; choose the kind of care before the person.
5. Woven circle: plum and ochre, typographic interlacing, communal introductions; warmth without clinical hierarchy.
6. Queensland verandah: generous white space and layered domestic photographs; familiar local welcome.
7. The sunroom: rich pine green, pale celery and rose, a light-filled photographic window; an open invitation to choose your care through people.

Seed 95c59d20 assigned candidate 7. The Sunroom is the build proposal.

Challengers judged on audience identification and product clarity:
- Silkscreen loft: declined on both; raises portrait consistency and equal care for all practitioners.
- Couture doll atelier: declined on both; raises individual profile art direction and deliberate margins.
- Fluid ink basin: competitive on calm identification, weaker on practitioner clarity; remains an alternate.
- Phosphor terminal: declined on both; raises keyboard clarity and unambiguous state feedback.
- Pickling calendar: declined on both; raises category labeling and useful filter behavior.
- Neon circuit: declined on both; raises clarity of the continuous route from discovery to contact.
